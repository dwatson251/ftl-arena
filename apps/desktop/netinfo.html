For friends on the internet, your router has to let them in on TCP port .

If that doesn't work: open your router's settings page, find Port forwarding (sometimes Virtual server or NAT), and forward TCP port to this computer's address (). Windows may also ask to allow FTL Arena through its firewall: allow it on private and public networks.
