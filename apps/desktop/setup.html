FTL ARENA

One-time setup. FTL Arena plays with your own copy of FTL: Faster Than Light (version 1.6 or later, from Steam, GOG or Humble): it reads the ships, weapons, art and sound from your install. Nothing from FTL comes with this app, and nothing is sent to other players.

It's the folder with ftl.dat in it, e.g. Steam\steamapps\common\FTL Faster Than Light (in Steam: right-click FTL → Manage → Browse local files). On a Mac, pick FTL.app.
