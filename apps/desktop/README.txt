FTL ARENA - 1v1 real-time FTL with your friends
================================================

You need your own copy of FTL: Faster Than Light (1.6 or later: Steam, GOG or Humble).
Nothing from FTL comes with this app: on first start it reads the game's data from your install.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop) and run "FTL Arena.exe".
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway"
   (the app isn't signed). Allow it through the firewall if asked.
2. First start: click "Choose my FTL folder..." and pick the folder with ftl.dat in it,
   e.g. C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\FTL Faster Than Light
   (in Steam: right-click FTL > Manage > Browse local files).
3. Play: "Practice vs bot" works straight away.

Playing with a friend
---------------------
One of you hosts (your app already is a server):
 - Host: menu FTL Arena > "Host a match..." shows the address to share and can try to open
   the port on your router automatically. If that fails, forward TCP port 8787 on your router
   to your computer. On the same Wi-Fi, the local address shown there works without that.
 - Friend: on the main menu, type the host's address (e.g. 81.2.3.4:8787) under Server, press
   Connect, then enter the host's match code and Join.
 - Host: "Create match", tell your friend the 4-letter code, build your ships, press Ready.

Your data (settings, the unpacked FTL files) lives in %APPDATA%\FTL Arena; delete that folder
to start over.
